1. MediaPipe Pose model (pose_landmarker_lite.tflite or pose_landmarker_full.tflite) — for body keypoints as a fallback when faces are partially occluded or in the watermark region. Also needed for the "body pose fallback" and "audio-visual speaker ID" strategies.

2. MediaPipe Face Landmarker (face_landmarker.tflite) — gives eye/nose/mouth landmarks for more precise crop centering than bounding boxes alone. Smaller ROI = better crop placement.

3. PySceneDetect (Python package) — for scene boundary detection in the video pipeline. Needed to split the 190s video into its 7 scenes before per-scene speaker tracking.

4. Speaker diarization / audio transcription — for the video pipeline's "scene-by-scene speaker tracking." Options:
   - Gemini 3.8-flash-live (has bidiGenerateContent — can process audio+video streams)
   - OpenAI Whisper (offline, CPU-capable with smaller models) + pyannote.audio for diarization
   - Lightweight alternative: FFmpeg audio extraction → librosa for basic audio analysis → Gemini for speaker ID from audio description

The watermark masking + body pose fallback also needs the pose model to detect "shoulders/waist" in the masked region — this is the key dependency I haven't resolved yet.
